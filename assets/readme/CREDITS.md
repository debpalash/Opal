## Fresh native UI previews

Captured on 2026-11-08 with Opal's production native shell, video surface, and
playback controls through its hidden-window capture harness. The harness uses
isolated demo state and paused frames, with no provider requests or personal data.
These are native UI previews, not recordings of a live streaming session.

- `sintel-player.webp`: Sintel trailer frame at 00:30.
- `bunny-player.webp`: Big Buck Bunny frame at 01:00.
- `connections.webp`: native Connect screen with no accounts connected.
- `sources/sintel-frame.jpg` and `sources/bunny-frame.jpg`: the corresponding film
  frames, extracted and encoded as JPEG. The preview renderer scales the images
  to the player viewport; the film imagery itself is otherwise unchanged.

The retained Sintel torrent recording (`../media/stream-a-torrent.mp4`) and its
poster (`torrent.webp`) use the same Sintel attribution. That recording predates
the current navigation. Older artwork remains in the repository for existing links.

Opal UI and logo follow the repository's own licensing. The film licenses do not
grant rights to Blender trademarks or imply endorsement of Opal.
