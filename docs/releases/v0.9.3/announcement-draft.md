Opal v0.9.3 brings browsing, playback and reading closer together.

On macOS: browser tabs and pinned extensions inside the app, YouTube → player handoff that keeps your position, comic-page discovery, and prompted background updates with Restart when you're ready.

Free, open source, no telemetry. Downloads for macOS, Linux and Windows alpha:
https://github.com/debpalash/Opal/releases/tag/v0.9.3

Try it and tell us what gets in your way. If it earns a place on your desktop, a star helps others find it.
